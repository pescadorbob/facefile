# User Receives Verification Email

**As a** new user, **I can** verify my email address by clicking a link sent to my inbox, **so that** my account is tied to a real, accessible email.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Verification email is sent immediately after registration
- [ ] The link expires after 24 hours
- [ ] A resend option is available if the email is not received within 5 minutes
- [ ] The user can log in and use the app while unverified, with a persistent banner reminder
