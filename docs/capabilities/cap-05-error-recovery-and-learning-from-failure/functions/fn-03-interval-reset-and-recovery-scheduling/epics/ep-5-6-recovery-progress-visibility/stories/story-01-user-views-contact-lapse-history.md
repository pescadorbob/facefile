# User Views Contact Lapse History

**As a** user, **I can** see the full lapse history for a specific contact, **so that** I understand how many times I've needed to rebuild that encoding and can decide whether a deeper structural repair is needed.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The contact detail page shows a timeline of all quiz events (date, rating, interval at the time)
- [ ] Lapse events are visually distinguished from successful reviews in the timeline
- [ ] The total lapse count is shown as a summary figure at the top of the history
- [ ] The history is scrollable and shows at least 50 events before paginating
