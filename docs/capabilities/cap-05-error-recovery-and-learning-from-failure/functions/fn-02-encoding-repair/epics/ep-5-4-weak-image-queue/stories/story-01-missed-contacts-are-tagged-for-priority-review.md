# Missed Contacts Are Tagged for Priority Review

**As a** user, **I can** have contacts I miss automatically tagged for higher-frequency review, **so that** they surface more often until the encoding stabilizes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Any contact rated "Forgot" is automatically added to the priority queue
- [ ] Priority-tagged contacts appear at the start of the next due-review session rather than in random order
- [ ] The tag is visible on the contact detail page as a "Needs Work" badge
- [ ] The tag is automatically removed when the contact achieves two consecutive "Good" or "Easy" ratings
