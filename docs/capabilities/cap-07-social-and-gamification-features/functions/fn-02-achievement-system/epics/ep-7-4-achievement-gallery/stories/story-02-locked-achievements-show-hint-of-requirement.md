# Locked Achievements Show a Hint of Requirement

**As a** user viewing the achievement gallery, **I can** see a hint of what each locked achievement requires, **so that** I have direction toward the next milestone without having the surprise spoiled.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Each locked achievement shows a one-line hint (e.g., "Complete 30 consecutive days of practice")
- [ ] Hints describe the requirement category without revealing the exact threshold for future higher-tier achievements
- [ ] Tapping a locked achievement shows the full hint in a detail modal
- [ ] The hint text is authored by the product team and not auto-generated
