# User Assigns Contact to Group

**As a** user, **I can** assign a contact to one or more groups, **so that** they appear in the right review sets and pre-event filters.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A group selector is available on both the contact creation and edit forms
- [ ] A contact can belong to multiple groups simultaneously
- [ ] Group membership is reflected immediately in the group's contact list
- [ ] Removing a contact from a group does not delete the contact or its review history
