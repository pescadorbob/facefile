# Application Timestamps the Encounter

**As a** user who has just added a contact, **I can** see when I met this person recorded automatically, **so that** I have a permanent record of the encounter without any extra effort.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] When a contact is saved, the application records the date and time of submission as the encounter timestamp
- [ ] The encounter timestamp is displayed on the person's detail view in the user's local timezone
- [ ] The timestamp is set at the moment the form is submitted, not when the form was first opened
- [ ] The timestamp cannot be edited by the user after saving (it is a permanent record of when the contact was created)
- [ ] The timestamp persists across sessions — it is visible the next time the user opens the contact
