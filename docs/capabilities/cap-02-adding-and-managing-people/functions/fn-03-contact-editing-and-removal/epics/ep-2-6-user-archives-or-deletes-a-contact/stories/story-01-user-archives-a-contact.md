# User Archives a Contact

**As a** user, **I can** archive a contact I no longer actively review, **so that** they are hidden from my active list and quiz sessions without being permanently deleted.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An "Archive" option is available from the contact detail or list action menu
- [ ] Archived contacts no longer appear in the active contact list or quiz sessions
- [ ] An "Archived" section or filter lets the user view and restore archived contacts
- [ ] Restoring an archived contact resumes their SM-2 schedule from where it left off
