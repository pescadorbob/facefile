# User Toggles Immediate Post-Add Quiz

**As a** user, **I can** enable or disable the immediate post-add quiz prompt, **so that** the workflow fits my pace when I'm adding contacts in bulk or under time pressure.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A toggle in quiz preferences enables or disables the post-add quiz
- [ ] When disabled, saving a contact goes directly to the contact list with no quiz prompt
- [ ] The toggle defaults to enabled for new accounts
- [ ] Disabling the toggle does not affect the contact's SM-2 schedule — the first review still happens on the next due date
