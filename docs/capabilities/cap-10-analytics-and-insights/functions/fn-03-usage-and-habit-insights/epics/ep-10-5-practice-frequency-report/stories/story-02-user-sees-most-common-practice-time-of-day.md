# User Sees Most Common Practice Time of Day

**As a** user, **I can** see the time of day I most often practice, **so that** I can confirm whether my reminder timing is well-matched to when I actually use the app.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A bar chart shows quiz activity grouped by hour of the day (0–23)
- [ ] The chart is computed from the timestamps of all quiz sessions in the past 30 days
- [ ] The peak hour is highlighted with a label (e.g., "You usually practice around 8 AM")
- [ ] If the peak hour differs from the configured reminder time by more than 2 hours, a suggestion to update the reminder is shown
