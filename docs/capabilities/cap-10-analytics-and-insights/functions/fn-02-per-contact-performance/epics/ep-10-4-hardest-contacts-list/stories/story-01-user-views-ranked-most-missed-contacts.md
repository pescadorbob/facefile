# User Views Ranked Most-Missed Contacts

**As a** user, **I can** see a ranked list of my contacts ordered by total miss count, **so that** I know exactly where to focus my encoding repair sessions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A "Hardest Contacts" view is accessible from the analytics or dashboard section
- [ ] Contacts are ranked by total "Forgot" ratings, with the most-missed at the top
- [ ] Each entry shows the contact's photo, name, miss count, and current review interval
- [ ] A "Start Repair Session" button launches a quiz with the top 5 hardest contacts
