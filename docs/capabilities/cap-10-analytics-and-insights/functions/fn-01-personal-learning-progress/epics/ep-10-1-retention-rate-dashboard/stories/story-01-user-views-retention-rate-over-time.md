# User Views Retention Rate Over Time

**As a** user, **I can** see my overall retention rate (percentage of quiz answers that were correct) over the last 7, 30, and 90 days, **so that** I understand whether my recall is improving, steady, or declining.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A retention rate chart is accessible from the progress or analytics section
- [ ] The rate is computed as (correct answers / total answers) × 100 for the selected window
- [ ] Three time windows (7 days, 30 days, 90 days) are selectable with a single tap
- [ ] The chart updates in real time after each quiz session completes
